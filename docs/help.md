### Who can open a link I copy?

**Copy link** keeps the current sharing settings. For a restricted item, only people who already have access can open it. Open **Share** to check who has access or let anyone with the link view it.

### How do I restore a deleted file?

In the browser where you deleted the file, open [Trash](#/trash) with the same account. Select **Restore** to put it back in its original folder. Trash keeps deleted items for 30 days.

### Which device has a copy of my files?

Open [Settings → Storage](#/settings/storage) and look under **Your devices**. Each device reports how much of your Drive it holds. **No report yet** means Drive is still waiting to hear from that device.
